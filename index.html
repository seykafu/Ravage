The Game

Where you stand decides the fight

Every attack depends on how both fighters are standing. A ready stance strikes back at whoever comes at it, and a brace takes the edge off a hit you can't avoid. Spears break shields, swords slip inside a spear's reach, and shields turn swords aside, so who you send matters as much as where you send them. When the fighting stops, a full visual novel takes over, with thirty painted characters, a story that splits seven ways, romance, jokes, and a twist or two you'll want to tell a friend about.

The Story

A visual novel between every battle

Between the fights, Ravage is an illustrated visual novel, and the tactics around it are as mean as we could make them. Thirty named characters plot, plead, fall in love and betray each other, with more than a hundred painted expressions between them. A lot of the campaign comes down to whether you trust the wrong person at the wrong moment.

The Hinge

One choice splits the story seven ways

Halfway through the campaign, Amar learns who his parents really are. A few days later, on the ship to the coast, he has to decide what to do about it. Five of the seven paths fight on to the end of the war, each with its own climax and its own finale. The other two walk away from the war, and the game lets them.

What's inside

How a battle works

There's no gacha and no menu buried inside another menu. You can explain each of these in a sentence and still be arguing about it an hour later.

I

The weapon triangle

Swords beat spears, spears beat shields, and shields beat swords. Bows sit outside the triangle and are the answer to dactyl riders. A late ally's lens ignores it too, and cuts through armor with focused light.

II

Stances and counters

Take a Ready stance to hit back at whoever attacks you, brace to take less damage, or stay neutral and swing first. The stance you pick changes every fight next to you.

III

Action points

Moving, striking and taking a stance all spend AP. You get two to four a turn, and a promotion raises that for good. Turns go quickly even when the decisions don't.

IV

Cover and terrain

Forest, rubble and obstacles change your defence and what you can see. Half of any plan is the map.

V

Promotions

Your fighters promote into Tier 2 classes mid-campaign. Each promotion brings a new look on the board, a new ability, and one more action every turn.

VI

Save mid-fight

Close the tab in the middle of a turn and the battle waits for you, exactly as you left it. Your saves stay on your own machine.

Where we are

The campaign is finished, and we're still polishing it

The whole game is live today: twenty-nine battles on a full run (thirty-five across all seven paths), seven endings, an eight-strong company for the endgame, promotions into every Tier 2 class, and a full soundtrack. What's left is polish.

Now

The full campaign, live

All of it, from the first battle to every ending on every path. You can suspend a fight mid-turn and pick it up later, and the first battle teaches you as you play. Every class and boss has animated sprites, the story has its own cinematics, bosses go out in finales, and the whole cast has painted portraits that change expression as they talk.
